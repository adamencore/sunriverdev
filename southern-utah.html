North of the border, south of heaven, right where you belong.

Known for near year-round sunshine, Washington County sits in the heart of Southern Utah's Color Country. Red rock vistas, warm desert temperatures and open space make SunRiver an ideal basecamp. SunRiver St. George hosts an annual art festival, golf tournaments throughout the year and portions of the Huntsman World Senior Games.

Live Out

Beauty as far as the eye can see.

Zion National Park

The most majestic national park in Utah, just 31 minutes from SunRiver Firelight.

Snow Canyon

Right in St. George's backyard, the area's most popular state park and a perfect place to hike.

Tuacahn

Broadway in the desert, in a stunning amphitheater set inside a red rock canyon.

Bryce Canyon

Crimson hoodoos, the spire-shaped rock formations you won't see anywhere else.

Las Vegas

Shows, dining and a weekend away, about 90 minutes from SunRiver.

Shakespeare Festival

Every summer in Cedar City, the Tony Award-winning festival stages world-class theater.

Lake Powell

Rent a houseboat with friends on the border of Utah and Arizona and unwind.

Moab

Home to Arches National Park, Delicate Arch, Balanced Rock and Devils Garden.

Grand Canyon

One of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World, a few hours from St. George.

Your options

Five ways to arrive.

The HECM for Purchase

The Home Equity Conversion Mortgage for Purchase uses a one-time down payment to buy a home, with no required monthly mortgage payments for as long as you live in the home. HUD insured.

The Bridge Loan

A short term option for buying a new home before selling your current one, without spending money on unnecessary fees.

The Senior Line of Credit

For homeowners 62 and older, an FHA insured HECM line of credit on a new purchase or an existing home, with flexible payments or no payments and access to funds over your lifetime.

The Retirement Loan

A low down payment loan built for seniors, with a payment that can be fixed for life and the security of owning your home.

The Recasting Loan

Buy now, then pay down the principal with a lump sum when your previous home sells and reset your monthly payment to where you want it, without new loan paperwork.

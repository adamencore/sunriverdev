Community Center

At the heart of SunRiver Firelight is a 17,000+ sq ft Community Center, the hub for social, recreational and wellness activities. Fitness rooms, event spaces and dedicated areas for hobbies and clubs give members a place to stay active, meet new friends and join community events.

SunRiver St. George

Where it all started.

Established in 1998 and nearing completion, SunRiver St. George is the original SunRiver community.

A 35,000+ sq ft community center, an 18-hole championship golf course, a restaurant and over 60 clubs and social groups have made SunRiver St. George a thriving home for resort-style, active adult living. SunRiver members have access to all of it, along with the friendships and quality of life that come with it.

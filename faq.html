You've got questions? We've got answers.

SunRiver is a hub of activity for the greater St. George area, from the annual kite festival and art festival to golf tournaments and portions of the Huntsman World Senior Games.

Local area

What is the weather like in Southern Utah?

Washington County has mild, low humidity winters and over 300 sunny days a year. The desert climate and 2,760 foot elevation support year-round recreation, including golf, tennis, fishing, water sports, hiking and bicycling.

What is the population of Washington County?

Washington County, including St. George, Hurricane, Santa Clara and Ivins, had a population of 197,680 in 2022.

What is the cost of living in Washington County?

The cost of living in Washington County is generally lower than the national average, with housing, utilities and health care standing out.

What outdoor activities are there in Washington County?

Washington County is a gateway to Arches, Bryce Canyon, Capitol Reef, Canyonlands, Grand Canyon, Zion and Lake Powell, plus many state parks. Red rock trails, three reservoirs, parks, sports complexes, indoor and outdoor pools and 13 public and private golf courses make it easy to stay active all year.

What is the predominant religion among SunRiver members and in Washington County?

About half of SunRiver members come from 20 different states outside the West, so religious affiliations are more diverse than in much of Southern Utah. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is the predominant religion in Washington County, and other faiths are well represented.

What is the crime rate in Washington County?

Crime is generally very low in St. George, with violent and property crime rates in Washington County well below the national average.

What is there to do for evening entertainment?

St. George offers more entertainment than many larger cities. Tuacahn Center for the Arts stages Broadway in the Desert each summer and concerts year-round. The Tony Award-winning Utah Shakespeare Festival runs June into October in Cedar City. The O.C. Tanner Amphitheater hosts a summer concert series against red canyon walls, and local theater companies, Utah Tech University's fine arts center and area high schools stage strong productions.

St. George also has multi-screen movie theaters, bowling and miniature golf. Las Vegas is about 90 minutes away and Mesquite, Nevada about 30.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Domestic animals are welcome, and SunRiver St. George even has a dog park.

Does SunRiver St. George have a homeowners association?

Yes. SunRiver St. George HOA dues are $172 per month. Homes in the Reflections gated community have additional private amenities, with an additional $70 per month in dues.

SunRiver St. George HOA, 4275 Country Club Drive, St. George, UT 84790. Phone (435) 287-3061. pmprollc.com

What do the HOA dues cover at SunRiver St. George?

Dues cover maintenance of all front yards, common areas, the community center, pool, sports courts and other amenities, plus association staffing and management.

Members may add rear yard maintenance for $54 per month or request walking mowers instead of riding mowers for $10 per month. Plant maintenance, irrigation repairs and rock refresh are available as needed for one-time fees.

What are the CC&Rs?

SunRiver St. George is subject to Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions that are actively enforced to keep a happy, healthy, beautiful environment for every SunRiver member.

Yes. The SunRiver Golf Course is public, with preferential rates and tee times for SunRiver members. The SunRiver Men's and Ladies' Golf Associations are active and always welcome new players. Call the Pro Shop at (435) 986-1000 for information or a tee time.

How We Build

Energy efficient homes

What is an energy efficient home?

A comfortable home is a high performing home, and that means being energy efficient. Since the early 2000s, SunRiver Construction has made energy efficiency a top priority in every home it builds.

HERS Rating

Measured, not guessed.

SunRiver uses the Home Energy Rating System Index, or HERS rating, on every home in the community. It is the industry standard for measuring a home's energy efficiency: the lower the HERS rating, the more efficient the home.

Thorough home testing

Tested for airtightness.

Home Energy Rater Kim Ewers consults with homebuilders on how to improve the overall performance of the homes they build. Blower door and duct leakage tests show how airtight construction is. Air that leaks out has to be replaced by outside air that must be cooled again, and lost A/C air is one of the biggest energy wasters in a home.
